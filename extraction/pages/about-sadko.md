# About SADKO Group — https://www.sadko-group.com/about-sadko/

## Main heading
`عن صادكو جروب` — About SADKO Group

## Company history

SADKO Group was **established in 1986** by engineer **Sadiq Hanna Ghobour** (صادق حنا غبور), a co-founder of **Ghobour Brothers Group** (مجموعة إخوان غبور, established **1961**).

The company specialises in **installment sales** across:
- Home appliances
- Electrical equipment
- Agricultural equipment transport vehicles

## Founding vision

To become **"the main distributor of Egyptian local industries."**

SADKO became the **exclusive distributor for Kiriazi (كريازى)** — positioned as the leader in durable, high-quality appliances in the Egyptian market.

## Core values

- Long-term, mutually beneficial customer relationships
- High-quality after-sales service
- Partnerships built on **"professional efficiency, fairness, respect, and trust"**
- Community service across all Egyptian socioeconomic levels

## Service coverage

Operates through **online sales and physical showrooms**, serving **over 40,000 customers annually** across:
- Greater Cairo
- Giza
- Alexandria
- The North Coast
- Marsa Matrouh
