# Policy pages

All three are **written in English** on an Arabic-first RTL site. This is a high-severity localization gap: the legal and trust content is unreadable to the primary audience.

---

## 1. Terms & Conditions — `/terms-conditions/`

### Introduction
The company reserves authority to modify terms at any time. Users must accept the conditions to access the site; continued use constitutes acceptance of updates.

### Use of the Website
- Membership requires users to be **18 or older**
- Account holders must provide accurate information and maintain password confidentiality
- Users bear responsibility for notifying SADKO of unauthorized access
- The company commits to treating personal data confidentially
- All internet commerce must comply with applicable laws

### Intellectual Property
> "All intellectual property rights…in the Site, information content on the Site and all the website design…shall remain our property."

Claims protection of site materials, design elements, and compilations under **Egyptian and international copyright law**.

### SADKO's Commitments
States it lawfully owns the materials offered and holds authority to sell them, complying with local and international regulations. Product descriptions are claimed accurate and truthful.

### Confidentiality
Acknowledges that while protection standards are followed, internet transmission **cannot guarantee 100% security** of personal information.

### Termination
Reserves unilateral rights to suspend accounts or discontinue services **without notice**.

## 2. Shipping & Payment Info — `/shipping-payment-info/`

### Shipping & delivery
> "Shipping and delivery is handled by Sadko crew staff to ensure maximum goods care and quality standard services."

**In-house delivery fleet** — a genuine differentiator that is under-marketed.

| Area | Delivery |
|------|----------|
| Cairo | **Free** |
| Giza | **Free** |
| Alexandria | **Free** |
| North Coast | **Summer months only**, fee set by customer service |

### Payment methods
- Cash on delivery (COD)
- Credit card

**Not offered:** installment plans (despite being the company's founding business model), bank transfer, mobile wallets (Vodafone Cash / InstaPay), or BNPL (ValU / Sympl) — all standard in the Egyptian market.

### Gaps
- No delivery **timeframes** stated
- No installation or old-appliance-removal service mentioned
- No delivery tracking reference

## 3. Return Policy — `/return-policy/`

### Return window
**14 days from delivery.**

### Conditions for acceptance
- "unopened, original box and/or packaging intact with all labels on it"
- Brand-new and unused
- Undamaged
- In identical condition to delivery

### Returns NOT supported when
1. The request exceeds the 14-day window post-delivery
2. The product shows signs of use, damage, or altered condition
3. The item is defective under **manufacturer's warranty** coverage (handled by warranty, not return)
4. The item has been used or installed
5. "original packing, freebies and accessories" are missing
6. The category is non-appliance: **books, handcrafts, games**

### Gaps
- **No refund timeline** stated
- **No refund method** stated (original payment vs. store credit)
- **No return process** — no RMA form, no pickup arrangement, no steps to initiate
- No return shipping cost policy
- Note: exclusion #6 references books/handcrafts/games, confirming the odd `حرف يدوية ومنتجات عضوية` (Handicrafts & Organic) catalog category

- No privacy policy or cookie policy page exists anywhere on the site (a GDPR/compliance gap for any international traffic).
