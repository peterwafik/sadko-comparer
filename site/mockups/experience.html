Let every visitor choose how to shop, and switch back with one press

SADKO has two ways to shop: the 3D house, where you walk through rooms and tap an appliance, and the classic shop, which is a normal product list with search and filters. Below are four ways to offer that choice. Each frame works: press the buttons inside it. Use “Start over” to see it again as a first visit. The screens are in Arabic as on the real site; the notes under each one give the English wording.

Recommended

The welcome card (idea 2), then one bottom bar that is both the room menu and the switch. The three rooms sit inside a little house drawn around them: a low V roof, a small chimney and walls, with “Home view” written inside the walls beside the rooms. Beside it is the “Shop view” button. Press “Shop view” and the rooms leave, the house squishes down to a small house, and the bar stays compact for as long as you are in the shop. Press the small house and it springs back open with the rooms inside.

1. A welcome page with two big doors

The first visit opens a full page with only two choices: large pictures, large words, one button each. Nothing else competes for attention. Choose one and you go straight in.

Wording

  • أهلاً بك في صادكو. كيف تحب أن تتسوق؟
    Welcome to SADKO. How would you like to shop?
  • تجوّل في البيت · Walk through the house
  • تسوّق بالطريقة العادية · Shop the usual way
  • تذكّر اختياري · Remember my choice

Strengths

  • The clearest option: it is impossible to miss.
  • Pictures explain the difference before any words are read.
  • Very large targets, good for older visitors and shaky hands.

Watch out

  • One extra step before shopping, so show it only on the first visit.
  • On its own it does not help someone switch later. Pair it with idea 3 or 4.

2. A welcome card over the moving house

The house loads behind a soft dark layer, so visitors already see what the 3D option looks like. A card in the middle asks the same question with two big buttons.

    How would you like to shop today?
  • تقدر تغيّر رأيك في أي وقت من الزر أعلى الصفحة
    You can change your mind any time with the button at the top of the page

Strengths

  • Lighter than a full page; the house still makes its first impression.
  • Takes less work to build: it sits on the current home page.

Watch out

  • Some people close pop-ups by reflex without reading them.
  • The house has to load first, which is slower on weak phones.

3. A labelled switch in the top bar, on every page

One control, always in the same place: “طريقة التسوق” (Way of shopping) followed by two buttons, each with an icon and a word. The current one is filled in teal and marked “أنت هنا” (You are here). One press moves you to the other experience.

Phone

Wording

  • طريقة التسوق: · Way of shopping:
  • البيت · The house  |  المتجر · The shop
  • أنت هنا · You are here

Strengths

  • Always visible, so nobody has to remember where it is.
  • Each option has an icon and a word, with no symbol-only buttons.
  • Works the same way on all 25 pages.

Watch out

  • Takes space in an already full top bar. Phones need a row of their own under the header.

4. A big floating button (desktop) and a bottom bar (phone)

Desktop: a large teal button in the corner names the other experience, for example “غيّر إلى: المتجر العادي” (Switch to: the classic shop). Phone: a two-tab bar at the bottom, where thumbs already rest, like the apps people use every day.

  • إلى المتجر العادي · To the classic shop
  • إلى التجول في البيت · To walking through the house

Strengths

  • The phone bar is the most familiar pattern for people who use WhatsApp, Facebook and other apps.

Watch out

  • The floating button can cover content. Keep it small on narrow screens and away from the cart.
  • The site already has a room bar at the bottom of the house; the two must not stack.

★ Welcome card + the house-shaped bottom bar

First visit: the welcome card asks the question over the house. After that, the bar at the bottom is the switch. In the home, the rooms live inside a house with a roof, chimney and walls, labelled “Home view”. In the shop, the house shrinks to a small closed house with a door, and no rooms are shown. Pressing it opens the house back up.

Phone

Try this

  • Choose an option on the card.
  • Press a room inside the house: the picture changes.
  • Press the amber “Shop view” button: the rooms leave and the house squishes down.
  • Press the small amber house to open it again.

Wording (English · Arabic)

  • Home view · عرض البيت (inside the walls, beside the rooms)
  • Shop view · عرض المتجر
  • You are here · أنت هنا  /  Go to · انتقل إلى
  • From here you move between the home and the shop
    من هنا تنتقل بين البيت والمتجر (shown once)

Why it suits older visitors

  • The house shape says “home” before any word is read, and the rooms are visibly inside it.
  • In the shop, the bar is small and calm: one house to go back, nothing else.
  • Teal means “you are here”, amber means “go there”, and both always carry words.
  • The house and the shop button never swap places.

Notes for building it

  • The motion lasts about 0.7 s and is skipped for people who turn on “reduce motion”. The chimney smoke stops too.
  • The shop's categories now sit at the top of the shop page, not in the bar.
  • It replaces today's room bar. Today's fourth button, “المتجر”, becomes “Shop view”.

How we would build it on the real site

  1. Remember the choice. Save sadko-mode = house | shop in the browser (localStorage, the same way the language choice is saved). There is no account needed.
  2. Welcome page. On the home page, if no choice is saved yet, show the welcome page before the house starts loading. Choosing “Shop the usual way” opens shop.html; choosing the house starts the 3D tour. The “Remember my choice” tick is on by default.
  3. Returning visitors. If the saved choice is “shop”, opening the home page goes straight to the shop. A link such as index.html?house always opens the house, so the switch and shared links keep working.
  4. The switch. Add it once in nav.js, the top bar shared by every page, so it appears on all pages with one change. On phones it becomes the bottom bar.
  5. Nothing is lost. The cart, saved items and language stay the same in both experiences, because they are already shared across the site.
  6. Measure it. Count how many people pick each door and how often they switch, using the analytics we already have, then adjust the default.
  7. Admin option. A setting in the admin portal can turn the welcome page on or off and choose which experience is the default.
